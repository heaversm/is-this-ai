How it works

First it reads the file for signed labels and "made by AI" tags. Then it runs SwinV2, a free detector, in your browser. Its download is saved, so later visits skip it, and checks take a second or two.

What it can't do

Detectors guess, and fakes made to fool people are the ones they miss. It can't read the hidden SynthID watermark itself, since only Google can, but it links to Google's checker. No label doesn't mean real: most apps strip labels on upload.

Where this comes from

A test of 363 images, 123 of them viral and fact-checked. The figures next to each detector are from that test: see the findings. The code is on GitHub. The models are converted from their open-source originals.

A research prototype, not a product. It can be wrong. Its "don't know" is an honest answer, not a failure.
