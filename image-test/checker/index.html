Checking…

Confirmed means proof found in the image: a signed label, a "made by AI" tag, or Google's watermark. Likely AI means the SwinV2 detector is at least 95% sure, which is a guess, not proof. There's no "likely real": in our test, detectors' "real" verdicts on viral images were wrong too often.
