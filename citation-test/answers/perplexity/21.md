The current U.S. federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour, as of November 13, 2026, for covered, nonexempt employees under the Fair Labor Standards Act.

When both federal and state minimum-wage laws apply, workers are entitled to the higher rate—so the federal minimum is not necessarily the minimum an employer can legally pay in your state. [dol](https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/wages)
