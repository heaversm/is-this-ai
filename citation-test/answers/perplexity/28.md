A traditional IRA can give you a tax deduction now if you’re eligible, but withdrawals are generally taxed in retirement. A Roth IRA is funded with after-tax money, so you get no deduction upfront, but qualified withdrawals—including investment growth—are tax-free.

In general, a Roth may be appealing if you expect your tax rate to be higher in retirement; a traditional IRA may suit you if you want a tax break now. [investor.vanguard](https://investor.vanguard.com/investor-resources-education/iras/roth-vs-traditional-ira)
