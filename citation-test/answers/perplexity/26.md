Credit scores are calculated from information in your credit report, and the result varies by scoring model, lender, and when it’s calculated—so you don’t have just one score.

For a typical FICO score, the factors are payment history (35%), amounts owed (30%), length of credit history (15%), new credit (10%), and credit mix (10%).  Personal details such as income, age, and where you live aren’t part of that FICO calculation. [myfico](https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/whats-in-your-credit-score)
